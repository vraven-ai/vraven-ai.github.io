What VRAVEN measures

  • Internal activations, gradients, attribution and information pathways
  • Observational evidence and intervention evidence as separate quantities
  • Stability, counterfactual and adversarial behaviour under declared constraints
  • Coverage, unresolved computation and limitations alongside every strong claim
Local by defaultYour model and tensors stay on your machine. VRAVEN does not add telemetry or upload model artefacts.
VRAVEN means Visual Reasoning and Activation Visualisation for Explainable Networks.
